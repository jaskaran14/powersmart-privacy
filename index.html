⚡ PowerSmart Ontario

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2025

📋 Summary

PowerSmart Ontario does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data stays on your device.

This policy explains what information PowerSmart Ontario accesses, how it is used, and what your rights are. We have designed this app with privacy as a default — no accounts, no tracking, no advertising.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

PowerSmart Ontario does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifier
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device ID, advertising ID, or phone number
  • Financial information of any kind
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

📱 Data Stored on Your Device Only

The app saves the following data locally using Android's SharedPreferences and local storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • App settings — theme preference (light/dark), notification preferences
  • Appliance run log — appliance name, date, duration, and estimated cost of runs you manually log
  • Routines — schedules you set for appliance reminders
  • Product catalog cache — a temporary copy of the affiliate product list (refreshed every 24 hours, stored locally)
  • Onboarding state — whether you have completed the intro tutorial
  • Season coach state — whether you have dismissed seasonal rate notifications

You can clear all of this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in Android Settings → Apps → PowerSmart Ontario → Clear Data.

🌐 Internet Access

The app requests internet permission for one purpose only:

  • Affiliate product catalog — the app periodically fetches a JSON file containing recommended product listings from a Google Sheets Web App or GitHub Gist hosted by the developer. No personal data is sent in this request — it is a plain HTTP GET request with no user identifiers.

Ontario electricity rates are calculated entirely on-device using built-in rules. No rate data is fetched from external servers.

🔔 Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local push notifications using Android's AlarmManager. These notifications are:

  • Generated entirely on your device
  • Not sent through any external push notification service
  • Not tracked or logged by the developer

You can disable notifications at any time in Android Settings → Apps → PowerSmart Ontario → Notifications.

🛍️ Third-Party Affiliate Links

The app displays affiliate product recommendations that link to third-party retailers (such as Amazon Canada, Best Buy, and others). When you tap a product link:

  • You are taken to the retailer's website or app in your browser
  • The retailer's own Privacy Policy applies from that point forward
  • We may earn a small commission if you make a purchase — at no extra cost to you
  • We do not receive any personal information about you from these retailers
  • We do not track which links you tap or whether a purchase is completed

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party retailers. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit. Current affiliate partners may include but are not limited to: Amazon Canada, Best Buy Canada, Home Depot Canada, and Canadian Tire.

👶 Children's Privacy

PowerSmart Ontario is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since the app collects no personal information from any user, there is no special risk to younger users.

✅ Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and no personal data is collected by us, you have full control at all times:

  • Access — all data is on your device; you own it
  • Deletion — uninstall the app or use Clear Data to remove everything
  • Portability — no data to export as none is held by us
  • Opt-out — deny internet or notification permissions at any time

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We will not introduce data collection without clearly communicating it in the app and updating this policy first.
